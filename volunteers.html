Volunteer Opportunities

Join our mission and make a hands-on difference in Uganda. We welcome volunteers who want to share their time, skills, and passion.

Whether you're a medical professional, teacher, builder, or simply someone with a heart to serve, there's a place for you at IGFM. Our volunteer programs offer life-changing experiences that will impact both you and the communities we serve.

Volunteer Opportunities Include:

  • Medical Missions: Serve at Berakhah Hospital and provide healthcare to those in need
  • Education: Teach at Berakhah School or help with educational programs
  • Childcare: Work with children at the Berakhah Childcare center
  • Construction: Help build churches, schools, and community facilities
  • Ministry: Participate in church planting, evangelism, and pastoral training
  • Administrative: Support our operations with your professional skills

Volunteer Requirements:

  • Commitment to our mission and Christian values
  • Minimum age of 18 years (or 16 with parental consent)
  • Ability to work in a cross-cultural environment
  • Flexible and adaptable mindset
  • Commitment of at least 2 weeks (longer-term preferred)

What We Provide:

  • Accommodation at our facilities
  • Meals during your stay
  • Local transportation
  • Orientation and cultural training
  • On-site support and supervision
