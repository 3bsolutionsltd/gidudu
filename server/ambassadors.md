Ann and Steve McKinley
During our 10 days in Uganda with Pastor Paul Gidudu and his team, our eyes were opened to their love and commitment to the children and people of their region. 

The vision is vast: train pastors, plant and build churches, adopt and educate children, reach prisoners with Christ’s love, and encourage believers with inspiring radio programs. 

The ministry exposes youth to faith and experiences that give them hope to escape poverty and fulfill God‘s purpose for their lives. We even connected with Ugandan lawyers involved in improving their justice system. We were stretched in our ability to minister to others. We invite you to support the ministry and personally participate in its work. It is both a privilege and a reward to do so.
Ohio USA
