Berakhah Childcare

Berakhah Childcare is dedicated to providing holistic care for vulnerable children in Uganda.

Berakhah Childcare provides comprehensive care and support for orphaned and vulnerable children across Uganda. Through our childcare program, we offer a safe, nurturing environment where children receive proper nutrition, medical care, education, and most importantly, the love and attention they need to thrive. Our goal is to break the cycle of poverty by investing in the next generation, helping children grow into healthy, educated, and Christ-centered adults who will transform their communities.

Through our childcare program, we provide:

  • Education and school supplies
  • Daily nutritious meals
  • Healthcare and medical support
  • Spiritual development and discipleship
  • Safe and nurturing environment
