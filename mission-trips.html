Mission Trips to Uganda

Join us on a life-changing mission trip to Uganda. Experience hands-on ministry, serve vulnerable communities, and be transformed by God's work in East Africa.

Our mission trips provide an opportunity to see firsthand the impact of IGFM's work while actively participating in ministry. Whether you're part of a church group, youth team, medical team, or individual traveler, we'll design a trip that aligns with your skills, interests, and God's calling on your life.

Mission Trip Activities:

  • Children's Ministry: Spend time with children at Berakhah Childcare, share meals, play games, and minister love and hope
  • Medical Missions: Serve at Berakhah Hospital or participate in mobile medical clinics in remote villages
  • Church Services: Preach, teach, lead worship, and encourage local congregations
  • Evangelism & Outreach: Share the Gospel in villages, schools, and prisons
  • Construction Projects: Help build churches, schools, or community facilities
  • Teacher Training: Equip local teachers and pastors with resources and training
  • Safe Water Projects: Install water filters and teach sanitation practices
  • Cultural Immersion: Experience Ugandan culture, cuisine, and community life

Trip Details:

  • Duration: 7-14 days (customizable)
  • Cost: Typically $1,800-$2,500 per person (includes lodging, meals, local transport, and activities)
  • Accommodations: Guest houses on ministry premises or local hotels
  • Group Size: 5-20 participants
  • Best Time: Year-round, with dry seasons (June-August, December-February) ideal for construction

What to Expect:

  • Life-changing spiritual experiences
  • Deep cross-cultural connections
  • Hands-on ministry opportunities
  • Beautiful Ugandan landscapes and wildlife
  • Warm hospitality from local communities
  • Challenges that will strengthen your faith

Trip Requirements:

  • Valid passport (6+ months validity)
  • Uganda visa (obtained on arrival or in advance)
  • Required vaccinations and travel insurance
  • Fundraising commitment (we provide support)
  • Pre-trip orientation and training
  • Heart to serve and learn
