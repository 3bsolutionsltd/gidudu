School Outreaches

Reaching students with the transforming message of the gospel through school outreaches.

School Outreaches bring the Gospel message directly to students in primary and secondary schools across Uganda. We believe that reaching young people during their formative years is crucial for building a strong Christian foundation that will guide them throughout their lives. Through chapel services, youth rallies, evangelistic campaigns, and mentorship programs, we share God's love with thousands of students, helping them understand their identity in Christ and make wise life decisions that honor God.

Our school outreach programs include:

  • Evangelistic assemblies and crusades
  • Bible study groups and discipleship
  • Character development programs
  • Distribution of Bibles and Christian literature
  • Follow-up and mentorship
