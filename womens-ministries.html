Women's Ministries

Empowering women to grow spiritually, support one another, and serve their communities with excellence.

Women's Ministries empowers women to discover their value and purpose in Christ while equipping them to thrive spiritually, emotionally, and economically. We create safe spaces where women can grow in their faith, receive biblical teaching, find healing from past wounds, and develop practical skills. Through fellowship, discipleship, and vocational training programs, we help women become strong, godly women who positively influence their families and communities for Christ.

Our women's ministry includes:

  • Women's fellowship and Bible studies
  • Widow care and support programs
  • Skills training and economic empowerment
  • Leadership development opportunities
  • Women's conferences and retreats
