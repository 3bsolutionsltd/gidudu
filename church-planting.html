Church Planting

We plant healthy, reproducing churches as God's primary tool for transformation in unreached communities.

Church Planting is central to our mission of spreading the Gospel throughout Uganda. We establish new churches in unreached communities, providing them with spiritual leadership, training, and resources to become self-sustaining centers of worship and discipleship. Each new church becomes a beacon of hope, transforming lives and communities through the power of Jesus Christ. Our goal is to plant vibrant, multiplying churches that will continue to reach future generations, creating a lasting impact for God's Kingdom in Uganda.

Our church planting strategy includes:

  • Identifying unreached communities
  • Training and equipping church planters
  • Establishing sustainable congregations
  • Developing indigenous leadership
  • Supporting new churches to maturity
