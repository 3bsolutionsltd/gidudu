Ambassador Program

Become an Ambassador

Join our Ambassador program and become a champion for vulnerable children and communities in Uganda.

As an IGFM Ambassador, you'll play a vital role in spreading awareness about our mission and mobilizing support in your community. You'll be equipped with resources, training, and ongoing support to effectively represent IGFM and make a difference from wherever you are.

What Ambassadors Do:

  • Raise Awareness: Share IGFM's mission and impact stories with your network
  • Organize Events: Host fundraisers, awareness events, or informational sessions
  • Engage Your Community: Connect churches, schools, and organizations with IGFM
  • Social Media Advocacy: Share our content and updates on your platforms
  • Build Partnerships: Connect us with potential donors and supporters
  • Pray: Intercede regularly for our ministry and the people we serve

Ambassador Benefits:

  • Direct connection with IGFM leadership
  • Exclusive ambassador resources and materials
  • Regular updates from Uganda
  • Ambassador training and development
  • Community of like-minded advocates
  • Recognition and appreciation for your service

Ambassador Requirements:

  • Passionate about IGFM's mission
  • Active in your local community
  • Strong communication skills
  • Willingness to commit at least 3-5 hours per month
  • Heart for serving vulnerable children and communities
